# Gallery recipes: visual intent before switches

Open **Gallery recipes…** in the studio. This offline catalogue explains all 24
visible entries in the [official gallery](https://xplanet.sourceforge.net/Gallery.html):
what the image is trying to show, view/command-line choices, per-body appearance,
resource/build prerequisites and the current editor's limits. Filter by title,
body, setting or prerequisite. The source URL is shown as text; browsing does not
launch a browser, fetch files, execute a command, render or change the draft.

Some entries offer **Browse related stock scene**. This opens the existing stock
library with a name filter, not the historical recipe. Inspect its summary and
choose **Open selected** explicitly, or go Back to retain the draft. A stock scene
is a starting point for a related technique, not a reproduction of the upstream
image. Opening a scene replaces the draft and resets session undo, exactly as in
the normal stock library; save or keep a recovery draft first if needed. Local
renderer selection stays unchanged. Stock sources and personal configuration
stay untouched; only an explicit later save creates a new native copy.

## What the gallery teaches

- **View is not appearance.** Target, observer, UTC, orientation, range and
  radius/FOV belong to the view. Maps, lighting, annotations and illustrative
  magnification belong to body configuration. Default inheritance and body scope
  matter; Earth clouds should not accidentally become every body's defaults.
- **Change one ingredient deliberately.** South America's two lighting views
  separate time from relief. The existing two-preview comparison can examine
  that distinction. Imagery-month comparison changes a texture, not the epoch.
- **Observer, target and orientation differ.** Body-centre viewpoints, a bounded
  lunar orbital observer and spacecraft trajectories are separate camera methods.
  An observer body's name does not imply a surface camera.
- **Visibility has physical and presentation causes.** Field of view, phase,
  projection and ring orientation can explain a missing body. Magnification is
  explicitly illustrative. Projection is not a multi-body space view.
- **A recipe has dependencies.** TLEs, origin rows, maps, fonts, prepared scattering
  tables, SPICE kernels and ephemeris coverage cannot be replaced by a filename or
  a switch. Capability diagnostics report evidence, not universal readiness.
- **Simplification must preserve choices.** Browsing this catalogue never changes
  active or inactive settings. The older Java configurator's
  [mode-switch warning](https://xplanetconfig.sourceforge.net/docu_faq.html) is a
  reminder not to regenerate only the settings a simplified screen understands.

## Evidence and limits

Reviewed November 17, 2026: all 24 visible gallery detail pages, their linked
local supporting configuration/data files, and 35 full-size images (including
rendered alternatives and real photographs). The images were inspected, not
redistributed in this repository. The catalogue is a paraphrased guide with
primary-source links; it does not bundle upstream image/data assets.

Historical commands were **not executed** in that research pass. Animation
archives, external kernels, commercial maps, old FTP URLs and author-local paths
were not established as available or usable. Some recipes require dates outside
our editor's conservative backend policy. Screenshots establish that an image
was produced, not the scientific accuracy of our build or a ready-to-run preset.
The browser does not add unsupported projections, arbitrary trajectories,
SPICE import, atmosphere preparation, per-body hiding or rich Pango editing.

The earlier capabilities work studied the manual, examples and selected renderer
source; it was not a systematic gallery review. This review complements it, not
replaces source-backed limits. The broader official README, README.config, FAQ,
OS X, Maps & Scripts, Clouds and Links pages were also surveyed. The upstream
[OS X](https://xplanet.sourceforge.net/OSX.php.html) and
[Links](https://xplanet.sourceforge.net/links.php.html) pages reference this project's earlier guide;
[Maps & Scripts](https://xplanet.sourceforge.net/maps.php.html) documents the wider
image/marker ecosystem behind the project.

## Next decisions, not automatic scope

Use these examples to guide manual feedback and choose a small visual experiment.
Test the complete open/change/preview/save/reopen/cancel loop before adding a new
capability. Advanced prerequisites remain in the
[astronomy assessment](advanced-astronomy-assessment.md); the browser does not
turn those research items into implemented features. General command import,
automatic downloads and wallpaper activation remain outside this slice.
