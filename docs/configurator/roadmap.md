save-as-new. See the [user guide](user-guide.md) for what you can do now and
[architecture](architecture.md) for the boundaries the project maintains.

Possible future areas include broader native-file inspection, richer
comparisons and sharing, and finite trajectory or atmosphere workflows.
They are not released features or schedule commitments. The
[gallery catalogue](gallery-recipes.md) shows what shipped examples can
teach; the [advanced astronomy limits](advanced-astronomy-assessment.md)
explain which renderer capabilities require additional evidence.
